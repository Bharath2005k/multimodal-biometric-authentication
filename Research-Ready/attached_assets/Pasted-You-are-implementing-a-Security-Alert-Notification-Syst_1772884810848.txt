You are implementing a **Security Alert Notification System** for a behavioral biometric banking authentication project.

Your task is to build a **backend module that automatically sends an email alert whenever suspicious or high-risk authentication behavior is detected**.

The alert must trigger when the system classifies a login attempt as **Suspicious Behavior, High Risk Authentication, or Duress Attack**.

---

# OBJECTIVE

Create a **real-time email alert system** that notifies the administrator when abnormal behavior is detected during authentication.

The email must be triggered automatically by the backend.

The user interface must **not show any indication that the alert was sent**.

This is a **silent alert system**.

---

# ALERT TRIGGER CONDITIONS

Send an alert when:

risk_score ≥ 60

or when classification is:

SUSPICIOUS BEHAVIOR
HIGH RISK AUTHENTICATION
CONFIRMED DURESS ATTACK
